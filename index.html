Who I Am

I'm Masiath Ibna Jamil, a final-year undergraduate at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology, studying Electrical and Electronic Engineering.

My focus is VLSI design and AI chipset architecture — understanding how carefully arranged transistors become intelligence. I work across every abstraction layer, from device physics to system-level AI accelerator design.

I believe great hardware engineers think in multiple dimensions simultaneously — physics, mathematics, architecture, and application — all at once.
