01 — About

Who I Am

I'm Masiath Ibna Jamil, a final-year undergraduate student at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), majoring in Electrical and Electronic Engineering.

My passion lies in VLSI design and AI chipset architecture — the art of translating algorithms into silicon. I'm fascinated by how hardware shapes the limits of intelligence, and I work to push those limits further.

I design and simulate circuits using industry-standard EDA tools, write HDL code in Verilog, and implement signal processing pipelines in MATLAB and Python. I believe great hardware engineers must think at every level of abstraction — from transistor physics to system architecture.

06 — Reviews

Book Reviews

Gödel, Escher, Bach
by Douglas R. Hofstadter
★★★★★
A book about consciousness, recursion, and the strange loops of self-reference. Hofstadter weaves together mathematics, music, and art in a way that changes how you think about thinking. Required reading for anyone who builds things with code.
Reviewed January 2026
The Structure of Scientific Revolutions
by Thomas S. Kuhn
★★★★★
Kuhn introduces "paradigm shift" and makes it stick. Reading this fundamentally changed how I understand progress — in science, in technology, in my own career. The ideas here are applicable far beyond academia.
Reviewed October 2025
Infinite Jest
by David Foster Wallace
★★★★☆
One of the most demanding and rewarding books I've ever read. Takes 200 pages to get going, but by the end you're devastated. Wallace understood addiction and entertainment in ways that feel prophetic for 2026.
Reviewed August 2025
